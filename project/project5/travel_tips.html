Travel Tips

Arizona is an amazing place to visit, but it’s important to be prepared for the desert environment and changing weather. Knowing what to expect, staying aware of your surroundings, and packing the right things can make your trip safer and more enjoyable.

Heat & Wildlife Caution

Arizona can get extremely hot, especially during the summer. Phoenix, Scottsdale, and Tucson can reach 100°F or higher, so make sure to drink plenty of water, wear sunscreen, choose lightweight clothing, and take breaks in shaded or air-conditioned areas.

You may also come across wildlife like snakes, scorpions, coyotes, and javelinas. If you’re hiking, stay on marked trails, watch where you step, and avoid getting too close to animals. Never feed wildlife, and always give them plenty of space.

Seasons

Arizona’s weather can be very different depending on where you are in the state. Northern Arizona, especially Flagstaff, can be much cooler than desert cities like Phoenix, Scottsdale, and Tucson.

Spring: Phoenix and Scottsdale start getting into the 80s and 90s, while Flagstaff is cooler, usually in the 50s and 60s.

Summer: Phoenix and Scottsdale can reach 100–110°F, while Flagstaff usually stays around the 70s and 80s. This is one of the biggest weather differences you’ll notice across the state.

Fall: Temperatures start to cool down, but Phoenix and Scottsdale can still reach the 80s and 90s. Flagstaff is much cooler and starts to feel more like fall.

Winter: Phoenix, Scottsdale, and Tucson usually have mild temperatures in the 60s, while Flagstaff can drop below freezing and get snow.

Important: Don’t assume the weather will be the same everywhere in Arizona. Temperatures can vary by 30°F or more between northern Arizona and the desert cities, so check the forecast for the specific area you’re visiting.

What to Pack

What you pack will depend on the season and where you’re going. For warmer areas, bring lightweight clothes, sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, and a reusable water bottle. Comfortable walking shoes are also a good idea if you plan on sightseeing.

If you’re visiting Flagstaff or traveling during the winter, bring layers, long pants, and a warm jacket. For hiking, pack extra water, comfortable hiking shoes, sunscreen, and a small first-aid kit.
